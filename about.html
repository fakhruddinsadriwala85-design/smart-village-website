About Our Project

Using information technology to build connected, accessible and digitally aware communities.

What is Smart Village / Smart City?

A Smart Village or Smart City uses technology and digital services to improve the everyday lives of citizens. The main purpose is to make information and essential services easier to access.

Information technology can support communication between citizens and local administration. Websites, mobile applications, digital forms and complaint systems can provide simple channels for accessing services and sharing community issues.

This project focuses on an IT-enabled community model where technology is used as a tool for better service accessibility, citizen participation and digital awareness.

Vision & Mission

The project focuses on using technology for practical community development.

👁️

Our Vision

To create a digitally connected community where citizens can easily access useful information, digital services and communication channels.

🎯

Our Mission

To demonstrate how simple IT solutions such as websites, mobile applications, digital awareness and online complaint systems can support community needs.

Project Objectives

Key objectives of the Smart Village / Smart City IT Enablement Project.

01

Improve Digital Access

Provide a simple digital platform through which citizens can find useful community information.

02

Improve Communication

Create better communication channels between citizens and service providers.

03

Encourage Digital Awareness

Help citizens understand safe and useful ways of using digital services.

04

Support Complaints

Provide a digital channel for submitting and tracking community complaints.

05

Promote Transparency

Make service-related information easier for citizens to understand and access.

06

Encourage Participation

Encourage citizens to provide feedback and participate in community improvement.
