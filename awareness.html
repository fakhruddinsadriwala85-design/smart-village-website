Digital Awareness

Learn how to use digital services safely, confidently and effectively in your everyday life.

Building a Digitally Aware Community

Digital technology can make community services faster and more accessible. Digital awareness helps citizens understand how to use online services, protect their personal information and make better use of available technology.

Digital Awareness Topics

Important areas that citizens should understand before using digital services.

💳

Digital Payments

Learn the basics of making digital payments and checking transactions.

  • UPI payments
  • QR code payments
  • Check transaction details
  • Never share your UPI PIN
🌐

Online Services

Understand how to access important public and community services online.

  • Online applications
  • Digital forms
  • Service portals
  • Download documents
🔐

Cyber Security

Protect your accounts and personal information while using the internet.

  • Use strong passwords
  • Enable security features
  • Avoid suspicious links
  • Report online fraud
📄

Digital Documents

Learn how digital documents can be stored and accessed when required.

  • Store documents safely
  • Scan important papers
  • Use digital copies
  • Keep backups
📱

Smartphone Skills

Develop basic smartphone skills for communication and digital services.

  • Install trusted apps
  • Manage settings
  • Use internet services
  • Update applications
📰

Digital Information

Learn how to identify reliable information available online.

  • Check information sources
  • Avoid fake news
  • Verify important information
  • Think before sharing

Stay Safe Online

A digitally enabled community also needs digital safety. Follow these simple practices whenever you use online services.

🔑

Protect Your Passwords

Do not share passwords or PINs with anyone.

📩

Be Careful With Messages

Avoid clicking unknown links or suspicious attachments.

📱

Keep Devices Updated

Install updates from trusted sources regularly.

🛡️

Protect Personal Information

Share personal information only with trusted services.

4 Simple Digital Habits

Small habits can make digital services safer and easier to use.

1

Verify

Check information before trusting or sharing it.

2

Protect

Keep passwords, PINs and personal details private.

3

Update

Keep your device and apps updated regularly.

4

Ask

Ask a trusted person when you are unsure about something.
